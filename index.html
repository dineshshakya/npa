Services

Traditional medicine for modern life. Your practitioner may draw from one or several complementary techniques:

  • Acupuncture — fine, sterile needles at carefully selected points
  • Cupping therapy — gentle suction complementing hands-on care
  • Gua sha — traditional hands-on technique
  • Moxibustion — warming technique for your care plan
  • Electroacupuncture — gentle stimulation when appropriate
  • Herbal medicine — traditional formulas for your health needs
  • Auricular therapy — focused treatment of points on the ear
  • Lifestyle guidance — practical nutrition and wellness tips
